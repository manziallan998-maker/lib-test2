2Who may use LibraryOS

  • Organisations such as schools, colleges and community libraries that we have approved. Each organisation gets its own separate workspace with its own school code.
  • Librarians can manage books, students, issuing, returns and reports for their own organisation. Viewers can only look at information, as the organisation allows.
  • Platform administrators at Billan Tec House Company approve registrations, verify payments and keep the system healthy.
  • Only people the organisation has authorised may hold an account. The organisation answers for everyone it gives access to.

  • Upload only files you are allowed to use. Do not upload files with harmful content or with data that does not belong in a library system.
